Saraswati Senior Secondary School has been part of the Bilaspur community since 1984. This website is a starting point for sharing the school’s story, people and learning environment.

Our story

Learning built on a strong foundation.

Saraswati Senior Secondary School is a co-educational school in Bilaspur, Yamunanagar district, Haryana. Established in 1984, it serves learners from the early years through senior secondary school.

The school’s public profile and reported activities offer a starting point for a fuller story about its students, teachers and community. This page can later be expanded with the school’s official history, mission, values, leadership message and campus photographs.
