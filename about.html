Saraswati Senior Secondary School

A school with roots, and room to grow.

Saraswati Senior Secondary School has been part of the Bilaspur community since 1984. This website is a starting point for sharing the school’s story, people and learning environment.

Our story

Learning built on a strong foundation.

Saraswati Senior Secondary School Bilaspur was established in 1984 and is listed as a private unaided, co-educational school in the rural Bilaspur block of Yamunanagar district, Haryana. It serves Classes I–XII and has an attached pre-primary section.

The school is recorded as operating from a private building, approachable by an all-weather road, with the academic session beginning in April. The supplied listing says it has not shifted to a new place and does not operate as a shift-school; it is listed as non-residential, and a ramp is marked as not required for classroom access. The school profile lists Hindi as the medium of instruction; the accompanying academic data also lists Hindi and English. This language detail should be confirmed with the school before publication.

What makes up the school environment?

The supplied school directory data describes classrooms in good condition, a separate office room for the head teacher, a library, a playground, functional tap-water access and a computer-aided learning lab.
