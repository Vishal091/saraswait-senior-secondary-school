Science & technology·6 February 2023

Students present science models and technology projects

A report published by The Tribune described a school science competition focused on machine learning and technology. Students presented projects and models, including an elevator, cooler, Newton’s laws of motion, volcano eruptions and the respiratory system. Principal Sube Singh Panjeta appreciated the participants’ work, and prizes were distributed to winners.

The report noted the school’s intention to organise the competition each year to encourage interest in new technology.
